A.NEWSPAPER INVASION.
An army of newspaper men passed
through Hamilton this morning. it was
made up of members of the National
Editorial Association from Chicago and
points west, bound for Asbury Park, New
Jersey, to attend the national con-
vention. The party numbered about
400 men and about half as many ladies,
and the train on which they travelled
was made up of twelve Pullman coaches
besides the necessary baggage cars.
Many of the travellers got acquainted
with quite a lot of citizens and were
treated handsomely here. They arrive at
Asbury Park on Sunday, spending part
of the two days at Niagara Falls and
in the Credit Valley. They were not in-
convenienced by the Pullman strike.

RESUMED RUNNING SOUTHWARD.
The Grand Trunk Railway has decided
to resume running the train due to leave
Port Dover at 6 a. m. and train due to
leave Hamilton at 2.15 p. m. for Port
Dover on July 3rd. These trains will run
on Tuesdays, Thursdays and Saturdays
until further notice. These trains will
also run on Monday, July 2nd.
