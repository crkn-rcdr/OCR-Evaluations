A Phenomenal Attraction Coming to
City Opera House Saturday,
August 9th.
Theatregoers certainly have a treat
ahead of them, and one that has been
looked forward to with much interest,
for Messrs. Fisher and Stevens will
present The Pink Lady, that most de-
lightful of musical comedies, which
has been the most-talked-of success
of the past and present seasons in
London and New York, at the City
Opera House, Saturday night, Aug.
9th.
The Pink Lady, more so than any
other play of its sort, is happy both
in its books and music. The music is
exceptionally fascinating, and such
numbers as Beautiful Lady, The Girl
by the Saskatchewan, The Kiss Waltz,
Donny Did, Donny Didn't, and Hide
and Seek, linger long in the memory.
A splendid cast will be seen here
with Olga DeBaugh, Elsa von Bostel,
Louise Kay, James McElhern, Georgie
Harvey, John Mundy, Russell Lennon,
Alfred Derry, Alice Moffatt, Lucie
Carter, Chas. J. Hines, Lee Townsend,.
Abbott Adams, Jack Hall, Mortimer
Meyer, and in fact many of the New
York and London favorites. Seats on
sale tomorrow at Ryan's drug store.
