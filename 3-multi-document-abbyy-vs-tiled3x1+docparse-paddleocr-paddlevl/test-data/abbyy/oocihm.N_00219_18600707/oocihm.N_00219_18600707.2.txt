Is our last issue we quoted a paragraph
from the Montreal Commercial Adver-
tiser, stating that important mineral
discoveries have been made in the rear
of this District, and that an exploring
party is fitting out to make further
search. We presume the statement is
correct, though we have not been able
to get any definite information either
concerning the discoveries or the pros
pectiveexploration, as the facts concern-
ing neither of them have yet transpired.
That there are vast mineral treasures in
those regions, however, is the opinion
of persons who profess to be skilled in
such matters.

That there is immense treasures lying
a few miles north of Three Rivers, re-
quiring nothing but enterprise and ener-
gy to develope, it is a fact. However,
which does not depend upon the disco-
very of mineral deposits. It is well
known that there is a large tract—al-
most an illimited* extent of land ad-
mirably adapted to cultivation, which
only requires to be opened up by suita-
ble roads, and subdued by the strong
arm of industry to furnish a comforta-
ble subsistence and a home for thou-
sands. Let these lands be surveyed

and brought into the market on reason-
able terms, and the requisite facilities
for their settlement be provided by the
Government, and it is not probable they
will be long allowed to lie waste.

Hitherto the bane of this part of the
country has been an undue dependance
upon the Lumber and Timber trade.
The land under cultivation has been
divided and sub-divided, until in most
cases the farms are mere patches, too
small to admit anything like a regular
and systematic rotation of crops; or
any other of the modern improvements
in agricultural science. And the sur-
plus population who could not find re-
munerative employment in the cultiva-
tion of the soil, instead of penetrating the
forest, clearing land and making homes
for themselves, have been in the habit
of spending their winters in the woods
lumbering and their summers in com
parative idleness. In this way they

had no personal redress. They were
not permitted even to purchase land.
