The 66th Order of 1850, already. given, allows the
defendant the option of moving to dismiss the Bill, or
setting it down to be heard. A cause may, under many
circumstances, be of such a nature that it is more to the
defendant's benefit to. have it disposed of on argument,
than put out of Court. Suits for account for instance,
where the defendant expects a balance in his favour; or a
bill by a vendee for specific performance, where the
defendant may be desirous of having the purchase com-
pleted, which has been hitherto delayed for want of the
production of the money by the Plaintiff; and numerous
other cases which might be mentioned.
It is provided, however, that the dismissal of the bill for
want of prosecution is a bar to a subsequent suit for the
same matter, unless the Court otherwise order.
