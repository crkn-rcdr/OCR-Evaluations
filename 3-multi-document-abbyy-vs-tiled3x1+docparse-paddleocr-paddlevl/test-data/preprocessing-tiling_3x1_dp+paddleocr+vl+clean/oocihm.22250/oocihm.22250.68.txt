The rules under which Plaintiffs and Defendants may
be, examined are as follow : :
Any party to a suit may be examined as a witness by
the party adverse in point of interest, or by any one of
several parties adverse in point of interest, without any
special order for that purpose: and may be compelled to
and subject to the same rules of examination as any other
witness, except as hereinafter provided. And any person,
for whose immediate benefit a suit is prosecuted or defend-
ed, though not a party to the record, may be regarded as
a party for the purpose of this order. Provided always,
that, where it shall appear upon the hearing that any par-
ty examined under this order is united in interest with the
examining party, the evidence so taken shall not be used
on behalf of either the examining party, or of the examining-
ant, but may be struck out on the hearing at the instance
of any party affected thereby.
