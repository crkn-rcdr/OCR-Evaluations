It will be remembered,(see ante, Chap.1, that the
Act of 12 Victoria, Chap.63, gives an appeal from the
decisions of the Court to the Court of Appeals composed
of all the Judges of the three Courts; and from that there
is an appeal to the Judicial Committee of the Privy Coun-
cil in England.
By the above mentioned Act the nine Judges were
empowered make rules regulating the conduct of Ap-
peals, and several rules were passed, laying down the
practice in appeals from all the Courts. These were
confirmed by 13 and 14 Victoria, Chap.51, and such of
them as relate to appeals from Chancery are subjoined.

Her present Majesty, Chapter sixty-three, shall be in the
form of a bond which, together with affidavit of Justifica
