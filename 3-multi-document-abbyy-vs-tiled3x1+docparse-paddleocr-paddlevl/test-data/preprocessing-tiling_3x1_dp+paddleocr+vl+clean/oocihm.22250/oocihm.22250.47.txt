The Ansmer.
The answer formerly consisted of a complete, literal
and substantial answer to the Interrogatories of the Bill.
'There are now no Interrogatories to the Bill,(except in
Bills for Discovery, as to which the practice is peculiar,),)
and the answer may be as short a statement of the defence
as the Defendant may think proper to put in. The
answer must be filed within one twenty-eight
days,— the first being inclusive, and the last exclusive
after appearance, unless further time be granted, which
may be done under certain circumstances on application
to the Master as provided by the following Orders. The
Order, by which the form of the answer is now governed,
is the 27th Order of 1850.
