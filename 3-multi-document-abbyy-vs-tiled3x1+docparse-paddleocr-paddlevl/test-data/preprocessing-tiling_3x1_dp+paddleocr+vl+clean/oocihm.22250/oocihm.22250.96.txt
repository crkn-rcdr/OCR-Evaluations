This branch of the jurisdiction will require some further
Orders to render the practice clear and certain.
The new rule on the subject is the following :
No Bill for discovery merely shall henceforward be
filed, except in aid of the prosecution, or defence, of "an
action at law.
" The Bill is commonly used in aid of the jurisdiction
of some Court of Law, to enable the party who prose-
cutes, or defends an action at law, to obtain a discovery
of the facts, which are material to the prosecution or
defence thereof." Story’s Equity Pleadings, sec.311.
