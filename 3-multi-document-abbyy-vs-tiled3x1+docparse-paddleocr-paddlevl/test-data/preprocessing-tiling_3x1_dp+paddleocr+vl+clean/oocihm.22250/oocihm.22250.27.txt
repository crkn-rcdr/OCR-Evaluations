der it advisable to alter or amend it. Some fact may
come to the Plaintiff's knowledgewhich it may be found
necessary to put in issue before the. Defendant has an-
swered the Bill; and at that early period the amendment
may be made-as of course. Or, after the Bill is answer-
ed, it may be found advisable to make an amendment
with a view to meeting the case set up in defence; and
frequently several amendments may become necessary.
