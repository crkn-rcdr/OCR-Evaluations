Proposal to Light City Hall...
At last evening's meeting of the
City Council in committee Ald. Farrell
submitted a proposition for lighting
the City Hall with electricity to be
generated by the city. He said the
Victoria Hospital trustees had for
sale a dy amo which had been used
at the hospital, and if the city would
purchase it they could use it to gen-
erate electricity to light the City Hall.
The question was discussed by sev-
eral aldermen, but the matter was re-
ferred to the light committee.
