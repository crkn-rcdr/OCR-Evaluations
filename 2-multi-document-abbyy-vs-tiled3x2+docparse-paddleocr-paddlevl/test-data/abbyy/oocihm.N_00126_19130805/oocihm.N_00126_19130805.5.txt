Miss Mae A. Sullivan had extorted

inherited $8,000,000 from the estate of
his father, the late Robert Hoe, manu-
facturer of printing presses, yesterday
filed an answer in the Supreme Court
denying that he had ever promised to
marry Miss Sullivan. He also denied
that he ever had agreed to maintain
her at the rate of $50,000 a year.

Miss Sullivan, who says she is nine-
teen years old. is plaintiff in two ac-
tions against Mr. Hoe, one being to
collect $150,000 for alleged breach of
promise of marriage, and the other to
collect $75,000 for eighteen months'
support.

In her first answer M #3 Sullivan

now suing she was only a little more
than fifteen years old when she met
Mr. Hoe.

The extortion charge which Mr. Hoe
now makes is the second which has
been made of this nature since the ac-
tion started. Miss Sullivan recently

announced her intention of starting an
action against certain persons with
whom she lived part of the time that
she was being maintained by Mr. Hoe.
She says that these persons threat-
ened Mr. Hoe and succeeded in fore-
ing him to pay between $15,000 and
$16,000 to them.
