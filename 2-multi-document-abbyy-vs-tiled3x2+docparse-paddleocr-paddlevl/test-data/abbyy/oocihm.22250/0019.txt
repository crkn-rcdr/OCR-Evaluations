An Act of last Session, 13th and 14th Vic. Ch. 50, dated
10th August, 1850, entitled, « An Act for the more
effectual Administration of Justice in the Court of Chan-
cery in Upper Canada," effects an important change in
this respect. It empowers the Judges to appoint Masters
•	and Deputy Registrars « in such localities as the said

-Judges may consider necessary and expedient for the
