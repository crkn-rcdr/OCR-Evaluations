of a secret society for the protection of
Protestantism in this Province. Through
out the Province Roman Catholics form
but one-sixth of the population; in tho
county of Brant but one-one-twelfth. If oc-
casion should ever exist for united action
in defence of Protestant principles, the
real leaders in the cause of evangelical
religion may be expected to take it
it
openly and above-board. There is n
possible occasion for any set of men to
band themselves together in secret so-
ciety, or to turn baiters of Romanists
in the name of the tolerant Nazaren.

that, in this Province at least, Romish
aggression is anything more than
a
bogey with which to frighten the timid,
the ignorant and the bigoted.

sake of Canadian nationalist Llism, there is
