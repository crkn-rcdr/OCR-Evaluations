section and in Hastings, Lennox and Ad.
dington, Frontenac, Prince Edward and

Edward farmers estimate that they will

Bush fires in some places continue to do
great damage to oropg and fencing. The

wrecked.south. of the. dark. tunnel.under.

Fatal Fall From a Window.
WINNIPEG, July 8.- R. A. Hasken, a well.
known Winnipegger, recently returned
from England, and formerly a member of
the firm of A. Colquhoun & Co., wholesale
liquor dealers, fell from a third-story
window of the Leland House Saturday
morning. He was instantly killed, his
head striking the sidewalk. His parents
live at Pontapool, Eng.
