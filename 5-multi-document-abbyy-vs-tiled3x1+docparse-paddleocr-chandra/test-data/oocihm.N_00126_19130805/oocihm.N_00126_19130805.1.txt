Horrible Atrocities of Band
Of Desperadoes in Mexico
Mexico City, Aug. 5.- How Matthew Gourd, an American
farmer, was lashed to a tree and forced to witness an attack on his
two shee-daughters by bandits, is a story brought here today from
Tampico.
The person relating the story said the bandits first requested
a cigarette from Gourd, who complied, and a peso was offered in
payment for them. Gourd was then tied and with a knife at his
throat was ordered to produce his money. He told his seventeen-
year-old daughter to bring the money from the house, and on her
appearance she was seized and together with her sister aged thir-
teen years, was mistreated. When the rebels left the women un-
tied the father, who reported the assault to the authorities at Tam-
pico. One version of the affair is that the assailants were not rebels,
but peons employed on Gourd's own farm near the Atascador
colony.
