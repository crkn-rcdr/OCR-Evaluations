The Thursday Half Holiday.
The following is a copy of the reso-
lution passed by the Labor Council at
its last meeting relative to the Thurs-
day half holiday and referred to in
The Gleaner yesterday:
Whereas, we regard the granting of
a weekly half holiday by the merchants
of Fredericton to their employees as a
boon to our fellow workers and a
movement showing consideration on
the part of employers for those whom
they employ.
Resolved, that this Council regards
the refusal of one large firm to grant
the half holiday as practically an ef-
fort to defeat the same, and we desire
to place on record our sympathy with
those who are promoting the holiday
movement, and pledge our support to
those merchants who have so geuer-
thosely considered the welfare of their
employees.
