The practice has been little, if in any respect, altered
by the above Order (84). The accounting party will be
compelled to bring in his accounts upon oath, and the
claimant will then base his charge upon such information
as the accounts may give him. And the accounting party
may be put on proof of his discharge. The charge will
not recapitulate the accounts on which the charging party
relies, but will merely state shortly the result.
