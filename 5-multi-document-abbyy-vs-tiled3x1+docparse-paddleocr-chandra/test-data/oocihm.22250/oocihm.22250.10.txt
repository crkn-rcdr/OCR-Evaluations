purchase money, but is in arrear as to the remainder. The vendor
brings an action of Ejectment-and turns the purchaser out of posses-
sion. The purchaser afterwards tenders his purchase money, but the
vendor refuses it, saying that the payment was not made in time, and
he will not now take it. He prefers keeping the part of the purchase

The right of the mortgager to redeem is no unreasonable indulgence,
