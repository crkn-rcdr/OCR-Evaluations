The cause being at issue, each party has two months'
time to examine witnesses, and a material alteration,
effected by the recent Orders, consists of the examination
by each party of the opposite party, if it is desired. The
time for taking evidence was formerly three months.
The rule to produce witnesses, formerly in use, is abolish-
ed. Written Interrogatories are now dispensed with.
