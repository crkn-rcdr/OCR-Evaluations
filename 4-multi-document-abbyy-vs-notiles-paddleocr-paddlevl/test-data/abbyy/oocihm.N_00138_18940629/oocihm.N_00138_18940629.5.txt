BUCHANAN THE RIB-STABBER.

The biggest political fraud in this
whole election campaign is not Dr. Ry-
erson, nor the calf with the cough, nor
the silver medal bull, nor even Muldoon
with his bogus whiskers, but Editor Bu-
chanan of the Hamilton Templar. He
beats them all. For years he has been
shrieking for prohibition, in season and
out of season, and at last when the
Mowat Government pledged its honor to
give prohibition to the very fullest meas-
ure that the law would allow, he turned
around and did all that he could to
knife the very men who compose that
Government. Ile could not have done
more if he had been the hireling of the*
Licensed Victuallers' Association, for his
paper went to the homes of prohibition-
ists where the regular organ of the Li-
censed Victuallers is never seen and
there did its insidious work in its sneak-
ing way. What encouragement is it to
any government to pledge itself to pro-
hibitionists when the organ of the party
stabs it in the back ? They say that Mr.
Buchanan wants to go to Parliament
and that he is a disappointed office-
seeker. That may be, but when a man
sets himself up to speak for a class he
should at least strive to be honest and
fair .This man howls against saloons,
but by his double dealing he has done
more harm to the prohibitionist cause
than fifty saloons, open night and day.-
